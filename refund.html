Last updated: 7 September 2026

1. General

This Refund Policy applies to services provided by TIPS Global Solution Sdn. Bhd. ("TIPS", "we", "us" or "our").

The specific refund, cancellation and payment terms applicable to a particular service may also be stated in the relevant quotation, order confirmation, invoice, service agreement or other written agreement with the customer.

2. Mobile Telecommunication Services

Refunds relating to mobile telecommunication services may depend on the nature of the service, activation status, usage, validity period and the terms applicable to the relevant telecommunications service.

Where a service has already been activated or used, any refund will be assessed based on the applicable service terms and applicable law.

3. Event Management Services

Event management services may involve advance bookings, venue arrangements, suppliers, equipment, manpower and other third-party commitments.

Cancellation charges or refundable amounts may therefore depend on the agreed event terms, cancellation timing and costs already incurred.

Where a separate event agreement or quotation specifies cancellation and refund terms, those terms will apply subject to applicable law.

4. Payment Solutions

Where TIPS provides or facilitates payment-related services, any refund or reversal will be subject to the applicable transaction, merchant, service and payment-provider terms.

TIPS does not guarantee that every payment or transaction will be refundable. Refund eligibility depends on the nature of the transaction, the applicable agreement and applicable law.

5. Refund Requests

To request a refund, please contact us as soon as reasonably practicable and provide:

  • Your name and contact details;
  • Relevant invoice, order or transaction reference;
  • The service concerned;
  • The reason for the refund request; and
  • Any supporting information reasonably required to assess the request.

6. Refund Assessment

Each refund request will be assessed based on the applicable service terms, the circumstances of the request and applicable Malaysian law.

Where a refund is approved, the method and timing of the refund may depend on the original payment method and any relevant payment service provider.

7. Statutory Rights

Nothing in this Refund Policy is intended to exclude, restrict or waive any consumer right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded or restricted under applicable Malaysian law.
